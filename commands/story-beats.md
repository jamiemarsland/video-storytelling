Load the `video-storytelling` skill and its references/script-template.md. Topic: $ARGUMENTS

1. Research the facts first and list the ones you'll rely on, with any uncertainties.
2. Answer the seven story questions. Propose two or three options for the person (real or a clearly invented everyday character) and for the plant, and pick one.
3. Fill in the beat sheet: narration lines written for the ear, 2–3 cuts per line, holds and sound cues.
4. Run the Story check and fix anything that fails before showing the result.
