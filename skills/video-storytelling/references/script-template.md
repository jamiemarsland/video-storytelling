# Beat sheet template

Fill this in before writing narration. Each beat lists: what is said · what is seen (2–3 cuts) · hold · sound.

```
TITLE:            (contains the subject's name and a promise, e.g. "...and the correct way to pronounce it")
QUESTION:         (what the viewer wants answered by second 10)
PERSON:           (name, age at each era, one look that stays the same: hair, glasses, a cardigan)
PLANT:            (the small detail, and the exact line that names it)
CLOCK:            (what counts down, from when to when)
TURN:             (the line where it goes wrong)
PAYOFF:           (the callback line + the promise kept)

COLD OPEN
  1. Concrete scene, a date, a strange act         · wide / detail / face      · 0.4s
  2. Short, short contrast ("Not books. Not flags.") · face                    · 0.3s
  3. The reveal in three words                      · detail                    · 1.2s
  4. Why the viewer should care today               · present-day picture
  5. Threat + "and it nearly worked"                · hands / face → BLACK      · 1.3s · title hit
  TITLE CARD (4–5s)

SETUP
  6. Bridge back in time                            · place / object            · ambience
  7. Meet the person (say if invented)              · portrait + name tag
  8. Their ordinary world, the frustration          · 2 cuts                    · 1.0s
  9–14. Explainer beats inside the story            · real thing → abstraction
  15. THE PLANT: "Remember those three letters."    · big type, clock appears   · 1.5s
  16. The first win / moment of wonder              · reaction shot             · 1.4s
  17. Bridge forward ("…For now.")                  · years flipping            · 2.4s silent

RISE
  18–21. Things grow, faster cuts, a silent montage · 6–8 cuts @ 0.5s           · music up

TURN
  22. Callback to the plant as a question           · same big type, big clock  · 1.0s · music drops to piano/tick
  23–27. Who, what, how much — one fact per line    · evidence + reconstruction
  28. The number, alone                             · big type                  · 2.2s
  29–33. The response and the loss                  · person, colour drains     · 1.8s

PAYOFF
  34. The clock hits zero                           · dawn                      · 0.6s · music returns warm
  35. The person recovers what they lost            · person
  36. Where it is now                               · present day, person tagged
  37. Callback to the cold open                     · cold-open image → today   · 0.8s
  38–40. Keep the title's promise; final line       · portrait                  · 2.0s
```
